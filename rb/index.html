Rb

Because trading statistics can be fun.

Trading roadbook

RoadBook (rb) is a trading journal: it keeps your trades with their setup, features, notes and charts, and tells you which features make trades work. Still in alpha, just born for personal use, provided as-is.

  • Trades grouped by year, month and week, with their totals, and a calendar by month or by year colored by result.
  • AutoFeatures, which reads the notes of the trades and checks the features named there.
  • Filters by instrument, setup, features, direction, day, hour and dates, plus a text filter with ! (not), | (or) and parentheses.
  • Stats and plots per result, day, week, month, setup, instrument and hour.
  • Charts for each trade, dropped on the trade form and read with OCR to fill in the trade. The graphics window can draw on them: lines, arrows, rectangles with FVG, IFVG and BRK presets, Fibonacci levels with OTE, and labels.
  • Backups of the roadbook and its charts.

The source, the user manual and the design document are at github.com/fjballest/rb. Binaries for macOS, Linux and Windows are in its releases.
